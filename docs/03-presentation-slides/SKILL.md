# Presentation Slides Skill

## Metadata
- **Skill Name**: `presentation-slides`
- **Purpose**: Distill a complex paper into a compelling 12-minute oral presentation.
- **Target Venues**: Conference Oral/Spotlight sessions.

## When to Use This Skill

- After your paper is accepted.
- When preparing for a lab meeting or seminar.

## Core Workflows

### Workflow 1: Paper to Slide Deck

1. **Extraction**: Identify the "Core Story" (Hook, Problem, Insight, Result).
2. **Storyboarding**: Plan the sequence of 10-15 slides.
3. **Content Generation**: Generate bullet points and figure placeholders for each slide.

## Slide Structure Template (12 min Oral)

1. **Title Slide** (1 min): Title, Authors, Motivation image.
2. **Introduction/Hook** (2 min): Why does this problem matter? Real-world example.
3. **The Gap** (1 min): Why do existing methods fail?
4. **Key Insight** (1 min): The "Aha!" moment of your approach.
5. **Methodology** (3 min): Diagram-heavy explanation. Minimal text.
6. **Experiments** (3 min): Key chart. Don't show giant tables.
7. **Conclusion** (1 min): Recap and Future Work.

## Style Guidelines

- **Text**: < 30 words per slide.
- **Visuals**: Every slide should have a visual anchor.
- **Animation**: Use build-ups to reveal complexity step-by-step.
